Shipping, Returns, Warranty & Privacy

Plain-English policies. Last updated: 2026.

Shipping Policy

We export worldwide from our factory in China. Freight cost and transit time depend on destination and order volume — message us on WhatsApp for a shipping quote with your inquiry.

  • Typical transit: 5–20 business days depending on destination and shipping method.
  • Options: express courier for samples, air freight for urgent orders, sea freight for container loads.
  • Tracking: every shipment includes a tracking number sent by email or WhatsApp.

Large items such as vanities and sanitary ceramics ship on reinforced pallets or in wooden crates.

Returns & Refunds

If your item arrives damaged or isn't as described, contact us within 30 days of delivery with photos and your order number. We will arrange a replacement or a refund according to what we agree together. Change-of-mind returns are accepted within 14 days for undamaged items in original packaging; return shipping is at the customer's cost.

Approved refunds are returned through the channel used for the order within 5–10 business days.

Warranty

All YUQING products carry a 5-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal residential use. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, improper installation, or hard-water scaling. To make a claim, reach us through the Contact page with your order number and photos.

Privacy Policy

This website is a product showcase. We collect only the information you choose to share with us — for example when you contact us by WhatsApp or email — and we use it solely to answer your questions, prepare quotes and fulfil any orders we agree on together.

We never sell your data, and we share it only with the logistics partners needed to deliver an order. If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data at any time through the Contact page.

This site uses no advertising cookies. Analytics, if enabled later, will be configured to run without cross-site tracking.

Terms of Service

Prices shown on this site are reference prices; final pricing, payment terms and delivery conditions are confirmed in writing when we prepare your quote. Nothing in these terms limits your statutory consumer rights.
