Our Story

Bathrooms, Done Properly

Who We Are

YUQING is a modern bathroom brand built on one simple belief: a great bathroom shouldn't be a luxury reserved for showrooms. We design, manufacture and ship premium faucets, toilets, vanities, sinks and shower systems directly from our factory to customers and partners in more than 50 countries.

Why Buy Direct

Traditional bathroom retail adds layers of importers, distributors and showrooms between the factory and your home — each layer marking up the price. By shipping direct, we deliver the same (often better) quality at a fraction of the price, and we stand behind every product with a 5-year warranty and responsive human support.

Quality You Can Verify

Every product in our catalog is produced in our own audited facilities and tested to international standards, including ISO 9001 quality management, CE marking for Europe, and water-efficiency certifications for the markets we serve. Before any item ships, it passes a 27-point inspection covering finish, function, packaging and completeness.

Serving the World

Over nearly three decades we have built an export network that reaches customers and partners on every continent — with flexible logistics options, full tracking, and export documentation handled by our own team.
